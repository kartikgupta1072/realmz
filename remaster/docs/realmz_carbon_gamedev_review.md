## 2. P0 - Must Fix Before Expanding Further

### P0.1 - Inverted Left-Handed Bitangent (`-Y` Normal Flip) on All Billboards & Cliff Walls
* **Evidence:** [CartographyBillboard.shader](file:///C:/realmz/remaster/RealmzUnity/Assets/Shaders/CartographyBillboard.shader#L300-L330), [BywaterElevationBuilder.cs](file:///C:/realmz/remaster/RealmzUnity/Assets/Scripts/Cartography/BywaterElevationBuilder.cs#L203-L280), [CartographyTerrain.shader](file:///C:/realmz/remaster/RealmzUnity/Assets/Shaders/CartographyTerrain.shader#L160-L167) and [L305-L307](file:///C:/realmz/remaster/RealmzUnity/Assets/Shaders/CartographyTerrain.shader#L305-L307).
* **Root Cause (Algebraic Proof):**
  1. **Billboards:** In Unity's left-handed world space (`+X` East, `+Y` Up, `+Z` North), when the camera looks North (`fwdXZ = (0, 0, +1)`), `rightXZ = (+1, 0, 0)` (`tang`) and `baseN` has `(0, +y, -z)` (`y > 0, z > 0`). On [CartographyBillboard.shader](file:///C:/realmz/remaster/RealmzUnity/Assets/Shaders/CartographyBillboard.shader#L329), `bitang = SafeNormalize(cross(baseN, tang))` evaluates `cross((0, y, -z), (1, 0, 0)) = (0, -z, -y)` - **pointing DOWN (`-Y`) and AWAY (`-Z`)**! Every OpenGL (`+Y` up) normal map generated by `laigter` on `bywater_inn`, trees, fences, grass blades, Knight, and Giant Frog has its vertical relief inverted (lit from underneath instead of overhead sun).
  2. **Cliff Walls:** In [BywaterElevationBuilder.cs](file:///C:/realmz/remaster/RealmzUnity/Assets/Scripts/Cartography/BywaterElevationBuilder.cs#L203-L218), `CheckAndEmitCliff` passes `topRight` and `topLeft` such that `tangDir = (topRight - topLeft).normalized` runs counter-clockwise around the plateau (`(-1,0,0)` on South cliffs, `(+1,0,0)` on North cliffs, `(0,0,+1)` on West cliffs, `(0,0,-1)` on East cliffs). For all 4 cliff directions, `cross(outwardDir, tangDir)` **already points `(0, +1, 0)` UP**, so setting `tangent.w = -1f` on [L279](file:///C:/realmz/remaster/RealmzUnity/Assets/Scripts/Cartography/BywaterElevationBuilder.cs#L279) makes URP's `GetVertexNormalInputs` multiply by `-1`, flipping `bitangentWS` to `(0, -1, 0)` **DOWN on all four cliff faces**! Worse, on South (`-Z`) and East (`+X`) cliffs, `tangDir` points `-X` and `-Z` while [CartographyTerrain.shader](file:///C:/realmz/remaster/RealmzUnity/Assets/Shaders/CartographyTerrain.shader#L160-L167) maps planar `+U` to `+positionWS.x` and `+positionWS.z`, inverting the horizontal `X` normal channel too.
* **Exact Fix:**
  * In `CartographyBillboard.shader` L329: change `cross(baseN, tang)` to `SafeNormalize(cross(tang, baseN))` (which yields `(0, +z, +y)`, pointing `+Y` up along the tilted card).
  * In `CartographyTerrain.shader` L305-L307: derive planar TBN directly from `absN` to match `ComputePixelLockedUV` (plateau: `T=(1,0,0), B=(0,0,1)`; `absN.x >= absN.z`: `T=(0,0,1), B=(0,1,0)`; `absN.z > absN.x`: `T=(1,0,0), B=(0,1,0)`), eliminating vertex tangent mismatch on cliffs completely.

### P0.3 - Coordinate-Axis Inversion (`-Z` vs `+Z`) & Flat `y = 0` Raycast Between `M1` and `Cartography`
* **Evidence:** [BywaterCameraRig.cs](file:///C:/realmz/remaster/RealmzUnity/Assets/Scripts/World/BywaterCameraRig.cs#L93-L96) and [L134](file:///C:/realmz/remaster/RealmzUnity/Assets/Scripts/World/BywaterCameraRig.cs#L134), [CartographyWorldData.cs](file:///C:/realmz/remaster/RealmzUnity/Assets/Scripts/Cartography/CartographyWorldData.cs#L81), [BywaterGridOverlays.cs](file:///C:/realmz/remaster/RealmzUnity/Assets/Scripts/World/BywaterGridOverlays.cs#L93-L258).
* **Root Cause:**
  * `M1` maps grid `(X, Y)` to `(X + 0.5f, y, -(Y + 0.5f))` (`+Y` South -> `-Z`), whereas `Cartography` maps `(X, Y)` to `(X + 0.5f, elevation, Y + 0.5f)` (`+Y` North -> `+Z`). Trying to drop `Cartography` into `M1_Bywater_Slice` without unifying `GridToWorld` flips North/South movement, camera bounds, and cliff shadows!
  * `BywaterCameraRig.ScreenPointToGridCoord` raycasts an infinite flat `new Plane(Vector3.up, Vector3.zero)` ([L134](file:///C:/realmz/remaster/RealmzUnity/Assets/Scripts/World/BywaterCameraRig.cs#L134)). At `48 deg` camera pitch (`cot(48 deg) = 0.90`), clicking a `+0.75m` ridge or `+1.50m` tier shifts the hovered tile by `0.68` to `1.35` meters (selecting the wrong tile!), while `BywaterGridOverlays.cs` draws hover/move/AoE quads at hardcoded `y = 0.022f..0.037f`, burying tactical highlights underground on elevated terrain and bridges.
* **Exact Fix:** Standardize on one canonical mapping (`GridToWorld(x, y) = (x + 0.5f, GetTileElevation(x, y), -(y + 0.5f))` so top-down map row `y=0` is North `+Z` and `y=height-1` is South `-Z`), raycast against `terrainMeshCollider` (`Physics.Raycast`) with flat-plane fallback, and offset `BywaterGridOverlays` vertices to `tileElevation + 0.025f`.

### P0.6 - `90x90` Scalability Wall & Native `Mesh` Memory Leaks
* **Evidence:** [BywaterFoliageScatter.cs](file:///C:/realmz/remaster/RealmzUnity/Assets/Scripts/Cartography/BywaterFoliageScatter.cs#L111-L137) and [L188-L195](file:///C:/realmz/remaster/RealmzUnity/Assets/Scripts/Cartography/BywaterFoliageScatter.cs#L188-L195), [BywaterElevationBuilder.cs](file:///C:/realmz/remaster/RealmzUnity/Assets/Scripts/Cartography/BywaterElevationBuilder.cs#L52-L57) and [L88-L95](file:///C:/realmz/remaster/RealmzUnity/Assets/Scripts/Cartography/BywaterElevationBuilder.cs#L88-L95), [CartographyAssetImporter.cs](file:///C:/realmz/remaster/RealmzUnity/Assets/Editor/Cartography/CartographyAssetImporter.cs#L272-L276).

  1. **Unculled Per-Frame `DrawMeshInstanced` Bus Flood:** `BywaterFoliageScatter.LateUpdate` re-populates `_propBlock` (`SetFloatArray`/`SetVectorArray`) and submits every batch across the entire map every frame with **zero spatial chunking or frustum culling**.   2. **Horizontal Cornfield Stripes in Meadow Scatter:** In `BywaterFoliageScatter.cs` L188-L195, `gridSide = 5` (`25` strata) uses `cellX = i % 5; cellY = (i / 5) % 5`.   3. **Unmanaged `Mesh` Leaks:** `BywaterElevationBuilder` allocates `new Mesh` in `BuildTerrainMesh` and `BuildWaterPlaneMesh` with no `OnDestroy()` cleanup and without destroying the prior runtime `Mesh` on `RebuildTerrain`. `CartographyAssetImporter` also leaves `Texture2DArray.Apply(false, makeNoLongerReadable: false)` readable in CPU RAM.
